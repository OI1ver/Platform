Platform 0.2.4 is the first GitHub-ready source release and remains an unsigned
technical beta for macOS 14 or later.

### Highlights

- Native Display, Extended and List departure-board modes
- Favourite stations, service calling points and station notices
- Clear stale, offline, delayed and cancelled states
- Custom app and menu-bar artwork
- Universal Apple silicon and Intel build
- No accounts, analytics or advertising

### Installation

Download `Platform-macOS-universal-0.2.4.zip`, move Platform to Applications,
then control-click it and choose **Open** on first launch. Do not disable
Gatekeeper globally. Full instructions are in the repository's installation
guide.

### Important

This build is ad-hoc signed but not Apple-notarised. Sparkle update archives are
separately protected with an EdDSA signature. Development copies of 0.2.4 made
before the GitHub feed was configured require one manual reinstall.
