Platform 0.2.5 improves the reliability and everyday behaviour of the menu-bar
board. It remains an unsigned beta for macOS 14 or later.

### What's improved

- The departure board now opens in a consistent position beside its menu-bar icon.
- Right-clicking the menu-bar icon provides quick access to refresh, Settings,
  About, restart and quit controls.
- Board, Settings and About windows now open reliably after switching or closing
  views.
- Launch at Login works correctly and explains when approval is needed in macOS
  System Settings.
- Settings navigation has more breathing room, About displays the current app
  artwork, and the board's rounded corners have been restored.
- Worker development tooling has been updated and its known dependency warning
  resolved.

### Installation

Download `Platform-macOS-universal-0.2.5.zip`, move Platform to Applications,
then control-click it and choose **Open** on first launch. Do not disable
Gatekeeper globally. Full instructions are in the repository's installation
guide.

### Important

This build is ad-hoc signed but not Apple-notarised. Sparkle update archives are
separately protected with an EdDSA signature.
