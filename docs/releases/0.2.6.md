Platform 0.2.6 improves live delay information and List-view readability. It
remains an unsigned beta for macOS 14 or later.

### What's improved

- Delayed departures now show `Exp HH:mm` whenever National Rail supplies an
  expected time. `Delayed` remains as the fallback when no estimate is
  available, and the reason for the delay still appears underneath.
- Station notices in List view use larger text that better fits the notice box.

### Installation

Download `Platform-macOS-universal-0.2.6.zip`, move Platform to Applications,
then control-click it and choose **Open** on first launch. Do not disable
Gatekeeper globally. Full instructions are in the repository's installation
guide.

### Important

This build is ad-hoc signed but not Apple-notarised. Sparkle update archives are
separately protected with an EdDSA signature.
