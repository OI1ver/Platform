# Changelog

All notable public changes to Platform are recorded here. Platform follows
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/) while it remains pre-1.0.

The exact source tree is retained from 0.2.4 onward. Earlier entries are a
conservative reconstruction from preserved application archives and development
records, not a claim that their original source snapshots are available.

## [0.2.4] - 2026-09-24

First release with a retained, publishable source tree.

### Added

- Complete open-source Swift client, Cloudflare Worker and shared documentation.
- Custom application and monochrome menu-bar artwork.
- Automated universal packaging, verification and signed Sparkle appcasts.

### Fixed

- Stale Worker responses now retain and identify the last successful board.
- Older station and service-detail requests can no longer overwrite newer selections.
- Extended mode uses its available space more clearly and scrolls information only for the featured service.
- View-selector state, highlighting and dismissal remain synchronised during rapid navigation.

## [0.2.3] - 2026-09-21

- Refined the connected view-selector shape and interaction feedback.

## [0.2.2] - 2026-09-21

- Corrected selector button interaction, hover feedback and transition behaviour.
- Refined List-mode arrival labels and station-notice layout.

## [0.2.1] - 2026-09-18

- Improved selector activation and alignment across the three board modes.
- Refined List-mode column positioning and status wording.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-09-18

- Added persistent Display, Extended and List board modes.
- Added the click-revealed three-mode selector.
- Added station-notice tickers and local-time footers to larger modes.

## [0.1.6] - 2026-09-18

- Added the dot-matrix service calling-point presentation.
- Compacted service details to match the primary board.

## [0.1.5] - 2026-09-18

- Refined board-to-service navigation and restoration of the original panel position.

## [0.1.4] - 2026-09-18

- Improved carriage diagrams for longer trains.
- Sanitised encoded and HTML-formatted disruption messages.

## [0.1.3] - 2026-09-18

- Refined marquee timing, initial pause and information transitions.

## [0.1.2] - 2026-09-18

- Corrected station, carriage and platform alignment.
- Ensured information marquees begin at the start of their content.

## [0.1.1] - 2026-09-18

- Early board layout and live-data refinements.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-18

- Initial preserved technical build of the macOS menu-bar departure board.
